## Notes

Chessing is a chess puzzle trainer made for small handheld screens. It includes 38,367 puzzles from the Lichess puzzle database in 10 difficulty levels and 19 themes, a rating that is saved between sessions, four board looks and sound effects. Rating, settings and the list of puzzles already seen are stored in `chessing/saves`.

Thanks to Lichess for releasing its puzzle database under CC0. Game and PortMaster packaging by Kaiman. Chessing is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Lichess.

## Controls

| Button | Action |
|--|--|
|D-pad / left stick|Move the cursor|
|A|Pick a piece and move it|
|B|Drop the piece / back|
|X|Hint|
|Y|Show the solution|
|R1|Skip to the next puzzle|
|Select|Back to the menu|
|Select + Start|Quit|

If A and B feel reversed on your device, open Controls in the main menu and press X to swap them.
